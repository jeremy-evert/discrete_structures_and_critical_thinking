# Week 2 gate

## End of Week 1

- Tuesday/Thursday material is source-backed and available through the selected delivery mode.
- The orientation-versus-logic/proof identity is durably recorded.
- A complete-package checklist names the selected sources and revisions.
- Any published due date has an approved cadence; no historical date is guessed.
- The deployment contract names source references, T/Th placement, target inputs, and dry-run safety.
- Assessment mode is explicit: frozen DSCT rubric/material contract plus automation, or manual review.

## Before Week 2 starts

The selected package has an instructor guide, ZyBooks selections, worked example,
activity, student write-up, observable rubric, topic exemplar, concept check,
checking step, DSCT AI-verification expectation, approved Coach material
manifest, source references for deployment, and matching student/instructor
copies. There is either a dry-run receipt for the intended path or an exact-
source manual delivery fallback with no shadow canonical copy.

Marker/Coach automation is conditional: if its DSCT adapter/material contract
is not ready, human review may run against the same frozen rubric without
changing the course design or promising automation.
