giving DSCT its own identity. Tuesday and Thursday each have a timed guide,
visible student artifact, and exit check. The source is printable/local and
does not depend on Canvas.

DSCT meetings. Formal DSCT topics, Week 2 lesson content, tools, grading, and
deployment were not started. The shared repository remained unchanged.

## Remaining yellow conditions

source-backed confirmation later. These do not prevent a local Week 1 launch.

## Verification conclusion

contract must reconcile the semester spine and author Week 2 separately; this
run stops here by design.
