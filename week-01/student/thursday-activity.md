# Thursday activity — Make your reasoning repeatable

**In class:** complete on paper or in a local file. This is practice; no point
value or LMS due date is assigned by this Week 1 package.

## Part 1 — Repair a help request

Turn this into a useful request:

> “My answer is wrong and I do not know why.”

Add an **Expected / Actual / Tried** record, the relevant source or rule, and
one concrete question. If you have no real problem yet, use the coin example
or a small class exercise.

## Part 2 — Evidence plan

Choose one small DSCT work session before the next class. Write:

- the question or outcome you want to reach;
- the source/definition you will consult;
- the first small step;
- the check you will perform;
- what file, note, output, or example will show what you tried;
- when and where you will do one focused 15–25 minute sprint.

Swap with a partner. Your partner should identify one hidden assumption and one
check that would make the plan stronger. Revise the plan.

## Part 3 — Week 2 readiness without guessing tools

Write one question you have about building a repeatable AI lab. The exact
setup instructions will be provided before Tuesday, August 25; this activity
does not require a particular provider, account, runtime, or local install.
