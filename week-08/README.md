# Week 8 — Induction, Recursion & Recurrences

**Tuesday, October 6; Thursday, October 8, 2026**

## Purpose and targets

This week asks: **where is the logical bridge, and are we assuming what we
are trying to prove?** Students distinguish a base case from an inductive or
recursive step, trace a terminating recursive definition, and identify a
missing base case or circular leap.

Prerequisite assumptions: students can state definitions and show a checkable
work trace from Weeks 4–7. The recurring method remains **Sources →
Rules/Assumptions → Work → Check → One-Sentence Summary**.

## Evidence path

- Tuesday technical evidence: `student/tuesday-activity.md`.
- Thursday: Show & Tell defense and individual report under
  `../assignments/show-and-tell-artifact.md`.
- Individual Odyssey evidence: `student/decision-gate.md`, this week's normal
  Decision Gate. It is distinct from the Show & Tell report but may refer to
  the same inspected technical trace rather than duplicate it.

## AI check

AI commonly supplies a recursive answer with no base case, an inductive step
that assumes the desired result for the next case, or a plausible trace that
never terminates. Check the smallest input, the reduction step, and one
nontrivial trace.
