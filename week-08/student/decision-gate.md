# Week 8 Decision Gate — Logical bridge evidence

Submit one individual, readable reasoning artifact using the five Week 1
headings. Use your recursion/recurrence trace or a comparable class example.

- **Sources:** definition(s) and claim used.
- **Rules / Assumptions:** domain, base case, and recursive/inductive rule.
- **Work:** trace or proof bridge with enough intermediate steps to inspect.
- **Check:** smallest case, termination argument, or counterexample attempt.
- **One-Sentence Summary:** what your evidence establishes and its boundary.

This is the ordinary Week 8 Decision Gate. It is not a second Show & Tell
report; refer to the same technical evidence when helpful instead of copying
it. Exact submission details are supplied when the course system is published.
