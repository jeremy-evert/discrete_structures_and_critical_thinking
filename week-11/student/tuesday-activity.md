# Tuesday activity — Does the relation earn its label?

Let `S = {1, 2, 3, 6}` and define `a R b` when `a` divides `b`.

## Sources and rules

Use divisibility plus the definitions of reflexive, symmetric, antisymmetric,
and transitive. List ordered pairs or build a matrix/digraph; do not infer
properties from the name “divides.”

## Work

1. Represent `R` as ordered pairs and as a matrix or digraph.
2. Classify all four properties. For each yes, use a definition; for each no,
   give a counterexample pair or chain.
3. Decide whether `R` is an equivalence relation, partial order, neither, or
   both, naming the required properties.

## Check and summary

An AI says divisibility is symmetric because 2 divides 6. Locate its broken
implication with a concrete pair. Check matrix/digraph agreement with your
symmetry and antisymmetry conclusions. Write a bounded classification with a
witness.
