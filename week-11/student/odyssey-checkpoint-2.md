# Reasoning Odyssey Checkpoint 2 — Relation-model evidence

This individual checkpoint is Week 11's **only** Odyssey submission and
one-third of the 15% checkpoint category. It replaces—not adds to—an ordinary
Week 11 Decision Gate.

Build on evidence from Weeks 8–11. Choose a relation/model question and use
this week's relation work to revise, test, or delimit a claim. Your context
may be a course example, network/category system, or optional carried world;
the formal domain and relation must be explicit.

1. **Claim and continuity** — current claim plus prior evidence it extends,
   revises, or questions.
2. **Sources and assumptions** — set, relation, and model choices.
3. **Formal work** — ordered pairs plus matrix/digraph (or equivalent), and
   property classification using proof, witness, or counterexample.
4. **Check and challenge** — test a condition that could break the label;
   identify material partner/AI help and independently check it.
5. **What changed and remains uncertain** — compare with prior evidence and
   name one model limit.
6. **One-Sentence Summary** — strongest supported conclusion.

The Pair Reasoning report is a separate social-reasoning object; do not paste
it as this checkpoint. Revision/resubmission follows `docs/grading-model.md`.
