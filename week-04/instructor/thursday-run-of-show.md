**75 minutes · 12:30–1:45 PM · Stafford 259**

| Time | Segment | Instructor move / visible output |
|---|---|---|

| 1:02–1:14 | Revision | Students repair a step, counterexample, or boundary after challenge and name what changed or survived. |
| 1:14–1:25 | Evidence receipt | Students complete the individual Show & Tell report and identify the separate Decision Gate artifact. |
