## Purpose and targets

This is the first formal-topic week. We turn an ordinary claim into precise
logical language, distinguish a valid argument from a merely true conclusion,
and support a conclusion with a proof or counterexample.

By the end of the week, you can translate a claim with propositions,
connectives, predicates, and quantifiers; identify an implication's
assumptions; and use a counterexample or short proof to check a claim.

**Bring forward:** the Week 1 sequence — Sources → Rules/Assumptions → Work →
Check → One-Sentence Summary — plus the repeatable-work habits from Weeks 2–3.

## AI fluency check

AI can make an invalid argument sound compelling. Ask: *does the conclusion
follow from the stated premises, or did the answer quietly add an assumption?*
Verify a proof step against a definition or defeat a universal claim with a
real counterexample. Fluent output alone is not evidence.
