# Thursday Show & Tell — Claim, challenge, revision

Prepare a small, inspectable logic artifact: either (a) a two-to-five step
proof of a conditional claim supplied in class, or (b) a counterexample that
defeats a universal claim. Your artifact must show definitions, assumptions,
steps, and a check.

In a small audience, give a brief public defense:

1. State the claim precisely.
2. Show the one proof step or counterexample on which your conclusion depends.
3. Name a limitation: what your evidence does not establish.
4. Invite one question about an assumption, inference, or case.

Then give one peer a professional critique. Critique the reasoning, not the
person: ask about a definition, a missing case, an implication direction, or
whether a counterexample is in the stated domain. Revise your artifact if the
challenge exposes a problem.

Submit the individual report using
`assignments/show-and-tell-artifact.md`; do not duplicate the technical
artifact in that report.
