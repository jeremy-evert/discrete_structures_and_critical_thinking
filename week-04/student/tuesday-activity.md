# Tuesday activity — Does it follow?

Work individually first; use paper or a local document. Keep your work: it is
the raw material for this week's evidence.

## A claim to inspect

Consider: “If a program passes all three examples below, it is correct for
every nonnegative integer input.” The examples are inputs `0`, `1`, and `2`.

1. Write the claim in an if–then form. What is the antecedent? What is the
   conclusion?
2. Name one hidden assumption that would have to be true for the conclusion to
   follow.
3. Give a counterexample program or rule that passes those three examples but
   fails at another nonnegative input. Describe the input and the actual
   output; code is optional.
4. Rewrite the original claim into a narrower claim that your evidence does
   justify.

## Make the reasoning inspectable

Use these headings in your notes:

- **Sources:** the definition of implication and the stated examples.
- **Rules / Assumptions:** what “correct” and “all inputs” mean here.
- **Work:** your translation, counterexample, or proof steps.
- **Check:** test the counterexample and ask whether it really defeats the
  universal conclusion.
- **One-Sentence Summary:** state exactly what the examples establish.

## AI fluency prompt

Ask an AI system (or inspect the supplied class example) whether the original
claim is valid. Record one sentence it offers, then independently identify the
premise or missing assumption that determines whether it is valid. Do not cite
the AI answer as proof.
