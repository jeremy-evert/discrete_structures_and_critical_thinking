# Week 4 Decision Gate — Validity needs a reason

Submit one individual artifact that answers a logic question supplied in
class. It may be a short proof, a precise argument analysis, or a
counterexample plus a repaired claim. It must be your own reasoning, even if
you discussed it publicly.

Use the five headings from `assignments/weekly-problem-solving-writeup.md`.
Your **Check** must either verify every stated proof step against a definition
or test that the counterexample satisfies the claim's hypotheses and violates
its conclusion. In the summary, distinguish “the conclusion happens to be
true” from “the argument is valid” when that distinction matters.

Use `templates/weekly-rubric.md` for the evidence standard. This ordinary
Decision Gate is this week's sole Odyssey/Decision-Gate submission.
