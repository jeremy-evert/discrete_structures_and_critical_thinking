The question is: **When a Farkle strategy appears better, what are we actually justified in believing?** Predict before inspecting the repeated evidence. Keep raw counts and denominators visible, then write a conclusion no larger than the evidence.

Your completed evidence receipt is a peer-visible graded discussion artifact. Classmates will see each other’s work as a shared learning resource.
