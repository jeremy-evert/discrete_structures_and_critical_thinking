# Week 16 Tuesday: Farkle Evidence Concept Map

The map is a reading path: start with the fixed comparison, then move from a claim to evidence, checks, and a bounded conclusion.

## Indented map

- Fixed comparison
  - Farkle rules and target score 4000
  - Strategy A: `learner:500`
  - Strategy B: `bank_at_425`
  - Ten games per seed
  - Balanced starts: five for A and five for B
  - Named seeds: 17, 6262, and 2026
  - Model assumptions: what stays fixed and what the model can see
- Claim
  - A precise statement to test
  - “Better” needs a criterion or decision rule
  - Scope says which rules, strategies, games, and seeds the claim covers
  - Prediction is written before the repeated evidence
- Evidence
  - Raw counts: games, starts, wins, and ties
  - Denominator: games counted for a rate
  - Win rate: wins divided by games
  - Percentage-point difference: Win A minus Win B
  - Seed makes a sample repeatable, not universal
- Check
  - Rebuild each rate from numerator and denominator
  - Compare the sign of A-minus-B in every row
  - Find minimum, maximum, and range
  - Ask whether an alternate metric changes the decision
  - Audit a confident reader or AI for hidden denominator, missing ordering criterion, one-sample proof, and model limits
- Conclusion
  - Defend the original limited claim
  - Revise it into a narrower claim
  - Refuse to declare a winner under the decision rule
  - State what the experiment still cannot establish
- Receipt
  - Sources
  - Rules / Assumptions
  - Work
  - Check Your Answer
  - One-Sentence Summary
  - Peer-visible graded discussion artifact

## Same map as a table

| Start here | Ask | Visible check | Safe next move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed comparison | What is held constant? | Target, labels, games, starts, and seeds are written | Record assumptions |
| Claim and criterion | What does “better” mean? | Another reader can apply the rule | Write a prediction |
| One sample | What happened in this row? | Wins and games rebuild both rates | Make a limited observation |
| Seed bundle | Does the pattern hold across rows? | Signs, minimum, maximum, and range are visible | Defend, revise, or refuse |
| Model limit | What is outside this model? | One limitation is named | Narrow the conclusion |
| Receipt | Can a peer inspect the trail? | Five headings are complete | Submit the peer-visible discussion |
