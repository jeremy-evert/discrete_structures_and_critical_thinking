# Week 9 — Counting & Combinatorial Reasoning

**Tuesday, October 13, 2026 · Thursday, October 15 is Fall Break — no class**

## Purpose and targets

Counting arguments only work when their cases are exhaustive and their
objects are counted exactly once. This Tuesday-only week uses sum/product
rules, permutations/combinations, and a small enumeration check to test that
discipline.

Students will choose a counting model, make the sample space/cases explicit,
find possible double counting or omissions, and explain what an enumeration
can and cannot check.

## Tuesday-only evidence path

Pair-style comparison is folded into Tuesday's activity; there is **no
Thursday meeting, Thursday artifact, Thursday reflection, or Thursday due
date**. The individual technical reasoning artifact in
`student/decision-gate.md` is this week's ordinary Decision Gate. It may
include the Tuesday partner challenge, but it is not a separate Pair Reasoning
report.

## AI check

AI can apply a familiar formula to the wrong object or silently count ordered
and unordered choices as the same. Ask it to list the cases, then check one
small instance by hand or enumeration.
