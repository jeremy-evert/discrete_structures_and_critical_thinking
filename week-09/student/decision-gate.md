# Week 9 Decision Gate — A defensible counting model

Submit one individual artifact based on the Tuesday activity or a comparable
counting problem. It must include:

- **Sources:** problem statement and definitions of the objects counted.
- **Rules / Assumptions:** whether order/repetition matters and why cases
  partition the outcomes.
- **Work:** your chosen sum/product/permutation/combination calculation.
- **Check:** a small enumeration, complementary count, or explicit
  double-counting/omission challenge.
- **One-Sentence Summary:** the result and why each outcome is counted once.

Include the Tuesday pair-style challenge if it changed or strengthened your
work. This is a normal Decision Gate; it is not a separate Pair Reasoning
report and has no Thursday artifact attached.
