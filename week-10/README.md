# Week 10 — Probability, Uncertainty & Evidence

**Tuesday, October 20 and Thursday, October 22, 2026** · COMSC-2043-1420

## Purpose and targets

How do base rates, independence, randomness, and simulation fool us? Model a
sample space; calculate conditional probability or expectation; and state
what a simulation checks and does not establish. Use **Sources →
Rules/Assumptions → Work → Check → One-Sentence Summary**.

## Meetings and evidence

- Tuesday: screening-test claim, conditional-probability calculation, and an
  AI base-rate check.
- Thursday: Show & Tell defense with a calculation and evidence table or
  simulation. Submit the individual reusable [Show & Tell report](../assignments/show-and-tell-artifact.md).
- Submit the individual [Decision Gate](student/decision-gate.md), the one
  Odyssey evidence submission this week—not an extra essay or Show & Tell copy.

No external account, paid service, or textbook is required. A table,
calculator, or local script is sufficient; an AI proposal is not evidence
until independently checked.
