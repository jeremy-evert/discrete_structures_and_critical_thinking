Buffon's needle, with hot dogs

A hot dog of length ℓ ≤ t crosses parallel lines spaced t apart when its center is within (ℓ/2)sin θ of a line. Average the allowed center distance over a uniform angle θ ∈ [0,π]: p = (1/π)∫₀π (ℓ/t)sin θ dθ = 2ℓ/(πt). Rearranging gives π ≈ 2ℓ/(t p̂).

The simulation uses spacing t=1 and hot-dog length ℓ=1. Its drawing uses a uniform random angle and trigonometry, which is appropriate for a computer demo. The rendered hot dogs are short line segments; crossings are gold.

Darts in a unit square

A quarter circle of radius 1 has area π/4 inside a unit square of area 1. Therefore p = π/4 and π ≈ 4 × (hits/N).

At least one shared birthday

For n people, multiply the no-match probabilities: P(no match) = 365/365 × 364/365 × … × (365−n+1)/365 = 365! / ((365−n)! 365ⁿ). Thus P(match) is one minus this product. At n=23 it is about 0.5073.

Run a simulation to see results.

Coupon collector

With n equally likely coupon types, when k distinct types are collected, the next new type arrives with probability (n−k)/n and expected wait n/(n−k). Summing waits gives E = n(1/n + 1/(n−1) + … + 1) = nHₙ.

Run a simulation to see results.

All simulations are local and deterministic for a given seed. Buffon's angle uses floating-point trigonometry, so Python and JavaScript estimates may differ at rare boundary cases.
