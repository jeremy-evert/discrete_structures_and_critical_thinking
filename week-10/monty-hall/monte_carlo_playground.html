Buffon's needle, with hot dogs

1. At angle θ, crossing requires center distance ≤ (ℓ/2)sin θ. 2. Average over center distance in [0,t/2]: P(cross | θ)=(ℓ/t)sin θ. 3. Average over θ∈[0,π]: p=(1/π)∫₀π(ℓ/t)sin θ dθ=2ℓ/(πt). 4. Rearrange: π≈2ℓ/(t p̂).

The simulation uses spacing t=1 and hot-dog length ℓ=1. Its drawing uses a uniform random angle and trigonometry, which is appropriate for a computer demo. Each hot dog is drawn as a rounded bun and sausage; crossings are gold.

Darts in a unit square

1. The quarter circle of radius 1 has area π/4. 2. The square has area 1, so p(hit)=π/4. 3. With p̂=hits/N, rearrange: π≈4p̂.

At least one shared birthday

1. After k distinct birthdays, the next person avoids a match with probability (365−k)/365. 2. Multiply for k=0,…,n−1: P(no match)=365!/((365−n)!365ⁿ). 3. Complement: P(match)=1−P(no match). At n=23 this is about 0.5073.

Coupon collector

1. With k types collected, a draw is new with probability (n−k)/n.
2. The expected wait for the next new type is the reciprocal: n/(n−k).
3. Add the waits: E=n(1/n+1/(n−1)+…+1)=nHₙ.

Secretary problem (n=100)

1. Skip r=⌊n/e⌋ applicants, remembering the best.
2. Then take the first later applicant better than every skipped applicant.
3. If the best applicant is at position j>r, success needs the best of the first j−1 to lie in the first r positions: probability r/(j−1).
4. Since the overall best is at j with probability 1/n, P(success)=Σⱼ₌ᵣ₊₁ⁿ r/[n(j−1)] → 1/e.
