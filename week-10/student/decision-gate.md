# Week 10 Decision Gate — Probability claim and evidence check

This individual artifact is Week 10's ordinary **Decision Gate** in the 30%
category. It is one submission, not a duplicate of the Show & Tell report.

Choose the screening example, a stated game/drawing process, or an
instructor-approved bounded claim. Use headings:

1. **Sources** — rates, counts, rules, or simulation input.
2. **Rules / Assumptions** — event definitions and independence,
   representativeness, or randomness assumptions.
3. **Work** — count table, calculation, expectation, or reproducible
   simulation.
4. **Check** — totals, second calculation, changed base rate, or
   simulation/model comparison; say what it cannot validate.
5. **One-Sentence Summary** — a conclusion no stronger than the evidence.

Identify AI help and independently check arithmetic and interpretation. Use
`assignments/weekly-problem-solving-writeup.md` as the reusable format guide.
