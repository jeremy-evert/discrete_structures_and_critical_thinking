## Policy boundaries

- No formal DSCT topic instruction or ZyBooks reading is assigned in Week 1.
- The five-part method is taught as a reusable habit, not a new grading rule.
- No points, grades, due dates, required AI provider, local model, container
  runtime, or new account are invented here.
- Students may use paper or local files. Canvas/Savnac is not required for
  in-room teaching; any later submission location remains to be confirmed.
- Week 2 is stated only as the resolved bridge: Tuesday Aug 25, Building Your
  AI Lab (adapted from CS2); Thursday Aug 27, Containers and Repeatability.
