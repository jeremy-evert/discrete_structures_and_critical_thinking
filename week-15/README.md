## What this week is for

Week 15 is an intentional pause between the formal-topic sequence and the
Week 16 Farkle + Machine Learning synthesis. There is **no new formal DSCT
topic**, normal class meeting, required assignment, or new due date this week.

Travel, family, work, rest, and access needs are real. Use the time in the
way that best helps you arrive at Week 16 able to reason from evidence.

  evidence-bearing work may be revised or resubmitted while the course is

  `docs/grading-model.md` for the settled policy and its course-system
  boundary.

- Read the Week 16 overview, `week-16/README.md`, so you know that its
  Farkle + ML evidence receipt asks for a bounded claim, repeatable evidence,
  and an honest statement of model limits.
