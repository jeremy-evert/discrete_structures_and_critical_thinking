# Career Evidence Pack Template

Use this repeated artifact pattern alongside a resume/profile:

- Target role and source link
- Claim or skill being demonstrated
- Evidence artifact/link (project, result, screenshot, repo, or document)
- STAR note: situation/task, action, result
- Gap or uncertainty
- Next action and date

This reflects the archive's progression from transcript/degree planning through resume, dream-job research, and gap analysis, plus spring 2026's emphasis on evidence rather than vague duties.
