### Grading Criteria

Work is graded on a pass-or-fail basis.

If you do not present in class, you will need to submit a document presenting your work. This document should include your code, the results from your code running, a brief discussion of how your results show that your code works, and any additional information. No code results means I assume your code does not run.

### Grading Rubrics

### For each weekly chapter homework assignment:

The main portion of this class is the process for working through a problem. Every problem should follow a similar process. For each question you answer, you should include five parts.

(20 points) A sentence or summary of what the question is asking for. (What is the big picture?)

(10 points) A list of references and resources to help answer the question. (What do other people think?)

(10 points) A brief description of general assumptions to help solve the problem, as well as key observations from the references, and a discussion of the gap between your references and your question. (How big of a jump are we making?)

(20 points) Your work shows your path from the question to your solution. [This is where your code and your results go] (Do some work)
