### Attendance Policy

Attendance will be taken regularly. If attendance will be a problem, please communicate with the instructor to identify solutions.

Life can be difficult. It is more difficult when you feel disconnected. We have regular meetings and fun interactions. I would like to help you feel checked in. Attendance will be taken at the start of every class. We will also be checking Canvas for the last activity. You are expected to be in class every day. You are also expected to log into Canvas every week to submit assignments, complete exams, or check for announcements. Perfect attendance (less than 3 absences and no skipped weeks in Canvas) will result in a one letter grade increase at the end of the semester. Six absences or two weeks of course inactivity will result in the reduction of a single letter grade, with an additional letter grade reduction after that for every week missed or additional 3 absences. If you miss three weeks or have 9 absences, the best you can hope for is a C. After four weeks of inactivity or 12 absences, the best grade you can earn is a D. Five weeks of course inactivity or 15 absences will be grounds for being withdrawn from the course.

The broader discussion on attendance is that many students learn more from each other than they do from the course material. This is not possible without your presence in the discussions.

Last semester, many of my students gave me feedback about my attendance policy. There was significant support for trying a semester with the cameras on. Basically, friends told me that when they shut the cameras off, they stopped paying attention. And several friends told me that when they had classes that required the camera to be on, they stayed more engaged. I was against a camera policy. I wanted to leave this open because turning on the camera means different things for different people. For some of my friends, it is as simple as not playing on your phone in class. For others it might mean significant prep time for appearance, and possibly even more time to travel to a location with good internet, and then feeling drained for the rest of the day. I want to be flexible with people and treat people with respect to make good choices. I am willing to work with individuals on a case-by-case basis if we need to make exceptions. In general, I would like to have cameras on for all students, both in the classroom and out in TV land. Our classroom has a classroom camera, but it is not as good as your own web cam. But if you can get on Zoom and participate in the class, you can be anywhere you want. My classroom is open to you, but you are not required to be physically there.

I am going to have a few tasks related to attendance. I ask you to do these things as active participants in your education. I will give more details in other areas. In general, if you are taking a class with me, I want you to think about what you need to do to graduate and what you need to do to get the career you are interested in.

### Late Assignment Policy

When you are trying to do this week’s work and then catch up on old work, you are going to be falling behind. Late work will be accepted for up to two weeks after the deadline with a 10% penalty imposed at the instructor’s discretion. This means I can choose to ignore the penalty. Work older than two weeks may not be accepted unless other arrangements are made. I can have some flexibility on this, but after about 2 weeks you are going to learn less, and the assignment will feel more like busy work. If you have extra time today, I would rather see you go for bonus points on the work due today. The goal of this policy is to help you stay involved and have a good semester, where every week you only have one major assignment due. But please know that the objective of this class is to work with you to help you develop a skill set.
