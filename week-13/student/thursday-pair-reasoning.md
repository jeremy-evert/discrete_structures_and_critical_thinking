# Thursday Pair Reasoning — Search claims need an audit

Bring your Tuesday tree or a supplied decision/search tree. Work with a
partner on one claim, such as: “This traversal will find every eligible
destination,” or “This decision tree makes a fair routing decision.”

1. **Before discussion:** Each person writes their current claim, the rule or
   evidence supporting it, and one uncertainty.
2. **Challenge the model:** Compare the original relationships with the tree.
   Locate one omitted edge, ambiguous split, duplicate case, or traversal
   assumption that could change the claim.
3. **Seek discriminating evidence:** Trace a case, add a missing node/edge,
   compare with a graph representation, or construct a counterexample. Do not
   settle a disagreement by voting.
4. **Revise or defend:** Update the tree/claim only as far as the evidence
   supports. A limited claim is a strong result when the wider one is not
   earned.

Each student submits an individual Pair Reasoning report using
`assignments/pair-reasoning-report.md`. Include the specific trace, definition,
or counterexample that mattered; “we agreed” alone is not evidence.
