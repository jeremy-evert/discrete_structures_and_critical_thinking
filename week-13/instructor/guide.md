# Week 13 instructor guide — Trees, Search & Decision Structures

**Meetings:** Tue Nov 10 and Thu Nov 12, 12:30–1:45 PM
**Thursday mode:** Pair Reasoning; individual report plus ordinary Decision Gate.

## Tuesday run of show (75 minutes)

| Time | Move | Evidence to protect |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | AI Fluency: show a plausible tree that omits one original relationship. Ask what was lost. | Students name a comparison check, not an AI verdict. |
| 0:08–0:20 | Professional Minds slot: discuss when a hierarchy is useful and when it hides accountability. | A professional consequence of an omitted relationship. |
| 0:20–0:35 | Teach tree vocabulary; contrast a rooted tree with a graph. | Root/edge meanings remain explicit. |
| 0:35–0:52 | Model preorder and breadth-first traces on the supplied tree. Intentionally make one trace error and correct it. | A visible, checkable trace. |
| 0:52–1:08 | Individual activity. Circulate for silently changed questions or unexplained leaf labels. | Tree plus omitted-relation audit. |
| 1:08–1:25 | Share a limited conclusion and preview Thursday's partner challenge. | One-sentence evidence boundary. |

## Thursday run of show (75 minutes)

| Time | Move | Evidence to protect |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:11 | Professional Minds slot: critique a claim rather than its author. | Specific, professional question. |
| 0:11–0:20 | Re-state Pair Reasoning: independent starting positions first. | No divided-labor substitute. |
| 0:20–0:52 | Pairs conduct the model audit and create discriminating evidence. | Trace, counterexample, or graph comparison. |
| 0:52–1:05 | Partners revise/defend and identify a remaining limit. | Claim is no broader than evidence. |
| 1:05–1:17 | Individual Decision Gate assembly and individual Pair Reasoning report. | Five-part artifact; report is not copied. |
| 1:17–1:25 | Exit: “What did the hierarchy erase, and did it matter?” | A concrete answer, not “complexity.” |

## Facilitation boundaries

Do not require coding, a particular diagramming tool, an external account, or
real sensitive data. If a student uses AI, ask for the original relationship
list and the comparison that checked the generated tree. Evaluate the
technical artifact with `templates/weekly-rubric.md` and the separate Pair
Reasoning report with `assignments/pair-reasoning-report.md`.
