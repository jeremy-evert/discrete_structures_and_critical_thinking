# Week 13 Tuesday: Trees, Search & Decision Structures

## What this week asks

What information disappears when a network becomes a hierarchy? This week uses
small, synthetic examples to compare a dependency graph, a directory-style
tree, and a decision tree.

Read the [Tuesday handout](../student/handout.md), [concept
map](../student/concept_map.md), and [glossary](../student/glossary.md) before
class. Then use the [Tuesday activity](../student/tuesday_activity.md) to
predict, trace, and audit a tree.
