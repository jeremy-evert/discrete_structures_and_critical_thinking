# Week 13 Tuesday: Trees, Search & Decision Structures

## What this week asks

What information disappears when a network becomes a hierarchy? This week uses
small, synthetic examples to compare a dependency graph, a directory-style
tree, and a decision tree.

Read the [Tuesday handout](../student/handout.md), [concept
map](../student/concept_map.md), and [glossary](../student/glossary.md) before
class. Then use the [Tuesday activity](../student/tuesday_activity.md) to
predict, trace, and audit a tree.

## Tuesday slides

[Open the Tuesday slide deck](../deck/trees_slides.pdf).

The slide prompts are also available as the [complete prompt

module. The prompts use synthetic data only and keep the Predict → Run →
Explain loop visible.

## How the prompts work

Paste each prompt into Claude Code, or your usual AI coding assistant, in a
scratch folder with Python 3 and pytest available. **Run** means ask the
assistant to execute the Python file or pytest command and show its result: you
predict first, compare the result with your prediction, and explain any
difference. For trace parts, paper-only work is the fallback.

Others in the course will see your work, and reading your classmates' work is part of the assignment. Use synthetic examples only.

Thursday continues the audit in the [Pair Reasoning
activity](../student/thursday_activity.md) and the existing [Decision
Gate](../student/decision-gate.md).
