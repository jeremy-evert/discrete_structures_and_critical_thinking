# Week 13 Decision Gate — A tree that earns its conclusion

Submit one readable individual artifact. You may draw it clearly on paper and
scan it, or use a local text/diagram format. No particular software is
required.

Choose a small supplied or personally invented **non-sensitive** search,
classification, or decision situation. Do not use private student, employee,
health, or financial data. Build a rooted tree with at least five labeled
nodes and make one claim it can support.

Use these headings:

1. **Sources** — identify the supplied scenario or explain the bounded
   invented scenario.
2. **Rules / Assumptions** — define what an edge and leaf mean; state the
   decision/search rule and relevant assumptions.
3. **Work** — show the labeled tree and two traversal or search traces,
   including one edge case.
4. **Check Your Answer** — test the claim using a second trace, a
   counterexample attempt, or comparison to the original non-tree
   relationships. Name one relation the tree leaves out.
5. **One-Sentence Summary** — state the conclusion and its limit.

The artifact is the Week 13 ordinary Decision Gate in the 30% Decision Gate
category. It is not an Odyssey Checkpoint and does not add a second technical
submission. Use `templates/weekly-rubric.md` as the evaluation shape.

The exact submission location and due time are published through the course
site; do not infer them from this source package.
