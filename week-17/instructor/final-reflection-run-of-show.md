# Week 17 instructor guide — final individual reflection

**Finals period: Dec. 7–11, 2026**

## Purpose and boundary

Use Week 17 to collect the settled 5% Final individual reflection. Do not
convert it into a comprehensive programming exam, Reasoning Defense, new
project, public presentation, or a demand that students re-complete prior
work.

## Release checklist

1. Publish `assignments/week-17-final-individual-reflection.md` and
   `assignments/week-17-final-individual-reflection-rubric.md` as the paired
   student source.
2. Make the course system's actual final-reflection window visible. Do not
   substitute a date guessed from this source package.
3. Remind students that two identifiable prior course artifacts are enough;
   they choose the evidence and need not disclose sensitive personal details.
4. State the AI boundary: AI may help with wording or organization only if the
   student checks it and gives an honest account; it cannot supply a student's
   personal learning evidence.

## Review approach

Read for an evidence-grounded account, not a performance of certainty. Full
credit can include a student who reports a remaining uncertainty, so long as
they identify a credible next check. Apply the rubric's five criteria; do not
score polish, preferred medium, or the amount of personal disclosure.

## Category integrity

Keep this object in the **Final individual reflection (5%)** category.
It must remain distinct from the Week 16 Farkle + Machine Learning Synthesis,
Decision Gates, and Reasoning Odyssey checkpoints.
