## Purpose and targets

Representations help us reason, but each representation also chooses what to
show and what to hide. This week we use sets, functions, domain/codomain,
composition, and sequences to inspect those choices.

By the end of the week, you can distinguish a relation from a function, state
a function's domain and codomain, trace a composition, and explain a
distinction a representation loses.

**Bring forward:** Week 4's precise definitions and counterexample habits.

## AI fluency check

An AI answer can silently change a domain, codomain, or meaning of an arrow
while preserving familiar-looking notation. Check every mapping by asking:
*what objects are allowed in, what objects are claimed out, and what cases did
this representation collapse?*
