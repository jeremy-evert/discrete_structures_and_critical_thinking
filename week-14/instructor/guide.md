# Week 14 instructor guide — Formal systems and model limits

**Meetings:** Tue Nov 17 and Thu Nov 19, 12:30–1:45 PM
**Thursday mode:** Show & Tell mini-capstone. Checkpoint 3 replaces the
ordinary Decision Gate; the Show & Tell report remains separate.

## Tuesday run of show (75 minutes)

| Time | Move | Evidence to protect |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | AI Fluency: display a plausible but unverified simplification. Ask what proof of equivalence requires. | Every assignment, not one favorable row. |
| 0:08–0:20 | Professional Minds slot: discuss confidence calibrated to a model's scope. | One phrase that limits a professional claim. |
| 0:20–0:38 | Build the supplied two-variable truth table and connect it to a simple circuit. | Operators and rows remain visible. |
| 0:38–0:53 | Introduce states, inputs, transitions, start state, and trace the badge machine. | Inputs are not confused with states. |
| 0:53–1:10 | Individual activity; ask students to find what the model cannot know. | A precise omitted state/condition. |
| 1:10–1:25 | Preview defense: a truth-table row or trace must be available to the audience. | Claim plus evidence boundary. |

## Thursday run of show (75 minutes)

| Time | Move | Evidence to protect |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:11 | Professional Minds slot: practice questions that expose assumptions without attacking a person. | Specific critique norm. |
| 0:11–0:22 | Explain the two distinct submissions and the no-double-Decision-Gate rule. | Checkpoint 3 and Show & Tell report are not conflated. |
| 0:22–0:53 | Small-group/public defenses. Require each student to show a row or trace and give a peer critique. | Inspectable evidence and critique given. |
| 0:53–1:08 | Quiet revision time: students test the challenge and narrow/defend their claim. | Revision based on an actual test. |
| 1:08–1:20 | Assemble Checkpoint 3 and individual Show & Tell reports. | Earlier-course connection plus current model boundary. |
| 1:20–1:25 | Exit: “What can your model establish, and what can it not?” | Limited conclusion. |

## Assessment and facilitation boundaries

Evaluate Checkpoint 3 with the five-part weekly rubric plus its synthesis
reflection. Evaluate the Show & Tell report with
`assignments/show-and-tell-artifact.md`. Do not demand programming, a
particular diagram tool, a required outside text, an AI account, or a polished
presentation. Students may use a self-contained non-sensitive example.

If time is tight, protect complete inspection of one small Boolean model and
one small state trace over covering many identities. Never accept “the tool
said equivalent” or “the diagram looks right” as the check.
