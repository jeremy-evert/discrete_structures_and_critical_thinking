# Thursday Show & Tell — defend a model, then name its boundary

Thursday is a small public defense. Classmates will see the work, so use a
fictional or otherwise non-sensitive example and critique the claim or model,
never the person.

## Choose one artifact path

- **Boolean/circuit path:** state one Boolean expression, a proposed
  equivalent expression or circuit, and a complete truth table for its
  variables. A Boolean expression is a rule that produces `True` or `False`
  from Boolean values.
- **State-machine path:** name the states, allowed inputs, transitions, start
  state, and two traces. A state machine is a bounded model whose transition
  rules say how an input changes the current state.

Keep the claim small enough that another student can inspect every row or
trace. Paper or a local editable file is enough; no account, software, or AI
tool is required.

## Defense procedure

1. State your claim and show the truth-table rows or traces that support it.
2. Name the strongest limitation before an audience has to discover it. A
   model limit is a relevant fact the model does not represent, so the model
   cannot establish it.
3. Answer one challenge by testing a row, input, state, or unstated
   assumption. If the challenge breaks the claim, narrow or revise the claim.
4. Give one specific, professional critique of a peer's evidence or model
   boundary. Critique the evidence, assumption, or inference—not the person.

## What to keep and what to submit

Keep four notes: the claim and best evidence, the strongest challenge, what
survived or changed, and the critique you gave. A revised or narrowed claim is
successful when the challenge earns it.

For Week 14, submit the individual [Reasoning Odyssey Checkpoint 3](../../student/odyssey-checkpoint-3.md)
using the published course-site submission details. The canonical Checkpoint 3
source replaces the ordinary Week 14 Decision Gate; do not create a second
Decision Gate artifact. The recurring Show & Tell report remains a distinct
course-work object when the course site asks for it, so do not duplicate it
inside the checkpoint. Use the checkpoint's Sources, Rules / Assumptions,
Work, Check Your Answer, One-Sentence Summary, and Odyssey reflection headings
for that submission.

## Exit sentence

Finish with: “My evidence establishes ___ under ___; it does not establish
___ because that boundary is outside the model.”
