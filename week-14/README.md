# Week 14 — Boolean Algebra, Circuits, SAT & Finite-State Machines

**Tuesday, November 17 and Thursday, November 19, 2026**
**Question:** Is this transformation equivalent under every assignment, and
what can this state model not establish?

Week 14 joins two formal systems. Boolean expressions and circuits describe
what is true for a fixed assignment. Finite-state machines describe how a
bounded model changes as inputs arrive. Both are powerful precisely because
their assumptions can be inspected — and both can sound more certain than the
model earns.

## Learning targets

By the end of the week, you can:

- check Boolean equivalence with a truth table and distinguish satisfiable
  from universally true;
- explain how a Boolean expression can be represented as a circuit;
- specify a small finite-state machine with states, inputs, transitions, and
  a trace; and
- state a model limit: a relevant condition, state, or transition the model
  does not represent.

## Tuesday: exhaust small cases

Use [the Tuesday activity](student/tuesday-activity.md) to verify a proposed

Prepare one compact model-defense artifact with
[the Thursday guide](student/thursday-show-and-tell.md). You will defend a
Boolean/circuit or state-machine claim, receive a challenge, and give a
professional critique of a peer. Submit:

1. your individual [Reasoning Odyssey Checkpoint 3](student/odyssey-checkpoint-3.md);
2. your individual [Show & Tell report](../assignments/show-and-tell-artifact.md).

Checkpoint 3 replaces the ordinary Week 14 Decision Gate. There is no Week 14
Decision Gate to submit. The Show & Tell report remains a distinct recurring
course-work object and should not duplicate the checkpoint.
