# Reasoning Odyssey Checkpoint 3 — What the model earns

This is the Week 14 individual Odyssey submission. It replaces the ordinary
Week 14 Decision Gate; do not create a second Decision Gate artifact.

Build a compact evidence package that connects this week's Boolean/circuit or
finite-state-machine reasoning to an earlier course claim, model, or carried
Reasoning Odyssey context. The connection must be real: if an earlier context
does not fit, use a clearly bounded new example and reflect on the transfer.

Use these headings:

1. **Sources** — identify this week's supplied/invented bounded scenario and
   the earlier course artifact or idea you are revisiting.
2. **Rules / Assumptions** — define the Boolean variables or machine states,
   inputs, and transitions. State what is deliberately outside the model.
3. **Work** — include either a complete small truth table/circuit check or a
   state diagram with two traces. Connect it to the earlier reasoning without
   pretending the two contexts are identical.
4. **Check Your Answer** — try to break the claim with a remaining assignment,
   input sequence, missing state, or counterexample. Say what survived and
   what did not.
5. **One-Sentence Summary** — make the strongest conclusion justified by the
   evidence and name its boundary.
6. **Odyssey reflection** — in a short paragraph, identify one way your
   reasoning has changed since an earlier artifact, one piece of evidence that
   changed or strengthened your mind, and one uncertainty you still carry.

Use `templates/weekly-rubric.md` for the five reasoning sections. The final
reflection is evaluated for honest evidence-bearing synthesis, not for a
particular tool, amount of code, polish, or choice of world/context. This is
Checkpoint 3 in the 15% Reasoning Odyssey checkpoint category; its exact
course-site submission details are published separately.
