# Show Your Work (Week 14, optional, no new deliverable)

By the end of this course you will have made something worth showing. This week connects to Thursday Show & Tell and Odyssey Checkpoint 3: you already state a limited claim and name its strongest limitation. Showing finished work to a stranger asks for the same discipline.

Read the one-page guide first: https://jeremy-evert.github.io/swosu-computing/show-your-work/

What to look for this week:

- **Claim and limit:** say what your work shows and what it does not, in plain words.
- **Evidence ladder:** one AI says it, two AIs agree, you read a source, you ran it, a qualified person confirmed it. Climb as high as the stakes demand.
- **Say how you made it:** name the tools, AI included, and what you checked yourself.
- **Other people's information:** no classmates' work or private details in anything public.

Before you publish anything, tick the checklist: https://github.com/jeremy-evert/oeqa_spell_roller_public/blob/main/CHECKLIST.md

Pick one thing you made, find the first checklist box it fails, and fix that one box. That is the whole task.
