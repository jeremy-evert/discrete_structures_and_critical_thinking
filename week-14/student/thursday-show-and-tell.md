# Thursday Show & Tell — Defend the model, then expose its boundary

Prepare a compact, inspectable artifact that makes one limited claim. Choose
one path:

- **Boolean/circuit path:** a Boolean expression, equivalent circuit or
  simplification, and a complete truth table for its variables; or
- **state-machine path:** a finite-state machine with named states, inputs,
  transitions, start state, two traces, and a stated limit.

You may connect the example to your Reasoning Odyssey context when it helps,
but a self-contained supplied or invented non-sensitive scenario is equally
valid. Do not use a peer's private data or claim a real system works this way.

During Show & Tell:

1. state your claim and show the truth-table rows or traces that support it;
2. name the strongest limitation before the audience has to discover it;
3. answer one challenge by testing a row, input, state, or unstated
   assumption; and
4. give one specific, professional critique of a peer's claim, evidence, or
   model boundary.

Then write the individual report in `assignments/show-and-tell-artifact.md`.
Critique the evidence or model, never the person. A revised or narrowed claim
is a successful outcome when the challenge earns it.
