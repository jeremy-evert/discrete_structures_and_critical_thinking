## 6. DSCT identity and AI expectation implemented

The package names DSCT as a reasoning course and uses “Reasoning Odyssey” to
frame the movement from question to defensible conclusion. Students practice
Sources, Rules/Assumptions, Work, Check, and One-Sentence Summary, plus
Expected/Actual/Tried for help requests. “Love AI more. Trust AI less.” is
concrete: AI is useful for exploration, but a fluent answer must be checked
against definitions, assumptions, steps, sources, tests, and conclusions. The
coin example demonstrates a convincing but overstrong claim without pulling
formal DSCT topic instruction into Week 1.
