# Week 17 Final Individual Reflection Rubric

**Category:** Final individual reflection — 5%

This rubric assesses evidence-bearing reflection and judgment, not eloquence,
personal disclosure, tool choice, or whether a student reports only success.

| Criterion | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---|
| Changed reasoning | Names a specific claim, method, or habit and explains a credible before/after change. |
| Evidence and checking | Identifies at least two course artifacts and explains how an observation, counterexample, test, definition, critique, or calculation supports the reflection. |
| AI judgment | Gives a concrete help/limit account and names an appropriate independent check or boundary. |
| Transfer and uncertainty | Connects one method to a bounded future context and honestly identifies an unresolved question or limit plus a next check. |
| Clear individual conclusion | Addresses the required final sentence and communicates an individual, evidence-grounded account. |

Revision may improve the reasoning or evidence trail under the course's
settled revision/resubmission policy in `docs/grading-model.md`; exact
system-window mechanics remain a deployment concern.
