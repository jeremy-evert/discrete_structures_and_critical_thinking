# Reusable Template: Career Artifact Sequence

**Historical basis:** 2022–26 recurring transcript/progress review, degree plan, resume, dream-job report, gap analysis, GitHub/LinkedIn, and professional learning/networking tasks.

Use a connected sequence: current academic/professional evidence → realistic degree plan → target role research → gap analysis → evidence-backed resume/profile. Keep the artifacts authentic, targeted to a real role, and explicit about next actions.
