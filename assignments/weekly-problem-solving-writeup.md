# Reusable Template: Weekly Problem-Solving Write-Up

**Historical basis:** 2021–22 homework and spring 2026 Weeks 1–14. Adapt the problem set and point value; do not copy semester due dates.

**Role in DSCT Fall 2026:** this is a reusable reasoning structure/template, not a second weekly graded object. A week's **Decision Gate** — the one recurring individual reasoning submission — may use this shape directly or adapt it into a more specialized artifact (see `week-16/README.md` / `assignments/week-16-farkle-evidence-receipt.md` for an example of a specialized Decision Gate).

Submit one readable artifact with headings: **Sources**, **Rules/Assumptions**, **Work**, **Check Your Answer**, and **One-Sentence Summary**. Show enough intermediate reasoning or code/output that another reader can reproduce the result. Cite outside help and explain what was learned from it.
