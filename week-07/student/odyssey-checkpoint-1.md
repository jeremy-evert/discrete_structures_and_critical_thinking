# Reasoning Odyssey Checkpoint 1 — Weeks 4–7

This is a compact individual synthesis of the reasoning evidence you have
made so far. It replaces the ordinary Week 7 Decision Gate; it does not add a
second Odyssey submission.

Use evidence from this week and at least two earlier formal weeks where
available. A proof attempt, counterexample, definition, trace, test, model,
or revision may serve as evidence.

1. **Claim trail:** identify one claim or method from Weeks 4–7 that became
   more precise after checking it.
2. **Evidence that changed or strengthened you:** name the specific proof
   step, counterexample, definition, trace, or test and explain its effect.
3. **Assumption boundary:** identify an assumption you now inspect more
   carefully. Connect it to this week's adversary/security question when it
   fits.
4. **Current conclusion:** state what your accumulated evidence justifies,
   and one thing it still does not justify.
5. **Next check:** describe the evidence that would most usefully challenge
   your current conclusion in a later week.

Use the Week 1 headings where they help. This is reasoning evidence, not a
new technical project and not an ordinary Decision Gate.
