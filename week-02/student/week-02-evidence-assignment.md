# Week 2 Evidence Portfolio — 40 points

This graded assignment is recurring weekly course work. It is not a
Reasoning Odyssey write-up, a final project, or a new semester grading
category.

## Submit

1. `local-ai-readiness.md`, following the shared local-AI readiness assignment
   and the DSCT extension. It must retain the accurate `READY` or `NOT READY`
   result, the shared answers, the DSCT reflection, and the privacy review.
2. Six searchable text files from the shared Container Connections manifest:
   `01_preflight.txt`, `02_container_identity.txt`,
   `03_named_volume.txt`, `04_bind_mount.txt`, `05_workload_hashes.txt`, and
   `reflection.md`.

The Tuesday and Thursday evidence can be submitted together as this portfolio.
Use the exact shared source instructions; this wrapper does not duplicate
their commands or technical prose.

## Points

- Tuesday local-AI readiness evidence: **20 points**.
- Thursday Container Connections evidence and reflection: **20 points**.
- Portfolio total: **40 points**.

This portfolio sits inside the **Weekly reinforcement / Reasoning Odyssey
gate** category (`docs/grading-model.md`, 30%) as Week 2's readiness/setup
entry — the substantive technical-evidence work for the week, the same role
that category plays for Weeks 3–14's formal Odyssey gates, even though Week
2 itself is setup rather than a formal Odyssey topic. It does not alter the
course split of 45% weekly Reasoning Odyssey write-ups, 50% recurring weekly
course work, and 5% final reflection.

## Acceptance boundary

An infrastructure-caused `NOT READY` Tuesday result or an instructor-directed
stop on Thursday is not automatically a zero. Preserve the observed evidence,
state what it proves and does not prove, and follow the instructor's approved
substitute or support path. Unsupported repairs, invented output, screenshots
without searchable evidence, Docker use, and universal determinism/portability
claims do not satisfy the assignment.

Optional World Bible notes may carry a student context forward, but they are
not required and are not graded in Week 2.
