# DSCT Week 2 full-rhythm map

The Tuesday/Thursday implementation is the course's meeting pattern. This map
names the seven campaign beats so the canonical package can preserve DSCT's
actual schedule while keeping evidence inspectable.

| Beat | DSCT object/path | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Monday AI Fluency | bounded claim/context warm-up in the Week 2 source map | claim, source, and limit note |
| Monday Local AI Lab | `week-02/student/tuesday/README.md` | Tuesday evidence/reflection receipt |
| Wednesday Professional Minds | Week 2 professional inquiry card | one learning-science claim connected to evidence practice |
| Wednesday Paired Reasoning | `assignments/pair-programming-report.md` | partner reasoning report |
| Friday Professional Minds | Week 2 professional inquiry revision | revised claim and limitation |
| Friday Show and Tell Reporting | `assignments/show-and-tell-artifact.md` | artifact and explanation |
| Sunday Reasoning Odyssey Pulse | bounded Sunday pulse | claim → evidence → what remains unknown |

The Monday/Wednesday/Friday/Sunday labels are campaign ordering labels; the
course's Tuesday/Thursday delivery remains authoritative. No new grading weight
or due-date policy is created by this index.
