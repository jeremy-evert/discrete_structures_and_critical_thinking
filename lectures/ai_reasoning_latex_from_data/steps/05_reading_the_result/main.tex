\section{What the evidence actually shows}With parallel limit 1, throughput approaches one stream: the GTX 1060 rises from 59.6 to 94.6 tokens/s as overhead is hidden, but it never demonstrates four requests generating at once. With limit 8, the T4 reaches 125.3 tokens/s at $N=16$ and then levels off. With limit 4, the GTX 1060 reaches 120.7 at $N=8$ and levels off. The knee is near the parallel limit, not automatically at the largest $N$.
