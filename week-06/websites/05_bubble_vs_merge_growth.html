Recursion was never the villain.

Bubble Sort is iterative and grows quadratically. Merge Sort is recursive and grows around n log₂n.

Bubble uses exact worst-case comparisons n(n−1)/2. Merge uses n·log₂n as a teaching model of growth, not an exact comparison count for one implementation.
