What happens when the input gets bigger?

Big O is a story about growth. Move n. Watch six common growth patterns separate.

Representative operation counts: 1, log₂n, n, n log₂n, n², and 2ⁿ. These are growth models, not measured runtimes.

Ask the room: If we double n, which lines barely care, and which ones explode?
