# Tuesday activity — Same places, different graph

Sites are `A`, `B`, `C`, and `D`. Travel links: A–B takes 2 minutes, B–C 2,
A–C 7, and C–D 1. A report says A–B is closed in the C-to-A direction only.

## Sources and assumptions

Treat times and closure report as source facts. Decide whether edges mean
physical connection, permitted travel, or travel time. State whether your
graph is directed and weighted; these choices change the answer.

## Work

1. Draw the responder's A-to-D graph after the closure.
2. Give a valid A-to-D path and total cost under your edge definition.
3. Model instead physical connection with direction ignored; state one changed
   conclusion.

## Check and summary

An AI uses A–B–C–D for both questions without mentioning direction. Decide
whether it is valid in each model and name its unstated edge meaning. Verify
every path step is an edge in your graph. State one bounded conclusion and its
edge assumption; keep this trace for the Decision Gate.
