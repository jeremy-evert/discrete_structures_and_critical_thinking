# Week 12 — Graphs & Network Reasoning

**Tuesday, November 3 and Thursday, November 5, 2026** · COMSC-2043-1420

## Purpose and targets

Are we solving the graph correctly, or modeling reality incorrectly? Model a
situation as a graph; analyze paths, connectivity, and traversal; distinguish
a correct procedure from a correct model. Use **Sources → Rules/Assumptions
→ Work → Check → One-Sentence Summary**.

The AI failure to inspect is a correct answer to the wrong graph or an edge
meaning that silently changes.

## Meetings and evidence

- Tuesday: convert a small travel/rescue description into a graph and compare
  two edge interpretations.
- Thursday: Show & Tell defense of a graph model and path/traversal claim;
  submit the reusable individual [Show & Tell report](../assignments/show-and-tell-artifact.md).
- Submit the individual [Decision Gate](student/decision-gate.md), the week's
  one ordinary Odyssey evidence submission.

A carried system may supply a network but is optional. Paper diagrams,
tables, or local tools suffice; verify AI-generated graph data before use.
