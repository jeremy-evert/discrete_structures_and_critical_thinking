# Week 12 Decision Gate — Graph model, claim, and boundary

This individual artifact is the Week 12 ordinary **Decision Gate** in the 30%
category, not a second copy of the Show & Tell report.

Choose a bounded network question from class or an instructor-approved,
non-sensitive source. A diagram, table, or program is acceptable.

1. **Sources** — facts/data, distinguished from graph choices.
2. **Rules / Assumptions** — vertices, edges, direction, weights, and
   objective.
3. **Work** — graph plus reproducible path/traversal/connectedness reasoning.
4. **Check** — challenge a modeling assumption, verify path steps, or compare
   another method; say whether it tests procedure, model, or both.
5. **One-Sentence Summary** — conclusion plus its model limit.

Label AI suggestions as suggestions and independently verify graph facts,
edge meanings, and calculations. Use `assignments/weekly-problem-solving-writeup.md`.
